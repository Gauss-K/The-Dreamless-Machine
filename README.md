# The Dreamless Machine & The Nameless Box

**A Historical and Philosophical Science Fiction Series**

**Author: Gauss-K (高斯)**

*Available in English and Chinese | 提供英文版与中文版*

## About the Series

*The Dreamless Machine* and its prequel, *The Nameless Box*, form a two-part novel series exploring the history of scientific discovery, the evolution of human knowledge, and the relationship between humanity and artificial intelligence.

Spanning centuries, the narrative moves from the Renaissance and the Scientific Revolution to Albert Einstein's era and a future shaped by intelligent machines.

Blending science fiction, historical fiction, and philosophical reflection, the novels explore scientific progress, the relationship between knowledge and power, human creativity, freedom, love, and responsibility.

At the heart of the series lies a question:

**When machines can think, reason, and discover, what does it mean to be human?**

## The Books

### I. The Dreamless Machine (不会做梦的机器)

**Main Novel | 正传**

From Einstein's pursuit of the fundamental laws of nature to a future transformed by artificial intelligence, the novel follows the intertwined lives of scientists, their families, and an extraordinary machine named Noah.

As physicist Lin Che searches for a deeper understanding of space, time, and quantum phenomena, the boundaries between human discovery and machine intelligence begin to shift.

The novel explores scientific ambition, the limitations of knowledge, and the enduring significance of human experience in an age of intelligent machines.

### II. The Nameless Box (没有名字的匣子)

**Prequel | 前传**

Beginning in the Renaissance, a mysterious box passes through the worlds of Leonardo da Vinci, Martin Luther, Galileo Galilei, and Isaac Newton.

Against the backdrop of printing, religious reform, and the emergence of modern science, the story explores how knowledge transforms societies and how scientific progress depends not only on great thinkers but also on countless ordinary individuals.

The prequel traces the historical and philosophical foundations of the world explored in *The Dreamless Machine*.

## Reading Order

The series consists of a main novel and its historical prequel:

1. **The Dreamless Machine** — Main novel, from Einstein to the age of artificial intelligence.
2. **The Nameless Box** — Prequel, from the Renaissance to Newton.

For readers who prefer chronological order, *The Nameless Box* takes place before *The Dreamless Machine*.

## A Note on Science and Fiction

These novels combine historical events and scientific ideas with literary imagination. Certain characters, conversations, events, and theoretical proposals are fictional.

Speculative scientific concepts presented in the novels are part of the narrative and should not be interpreted as established scientific conclusions.
